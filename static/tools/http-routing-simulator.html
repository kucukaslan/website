What the persistent lines mean
They are already-established TCP connections and remain between requests. ClusterIP flows keep a connection color from the client through its fixed pod; the connection badge states that pin explicitly. Gray Ingress connections terminate at the proxy.
Why the Ingress side is different
The client connection ends at the L7 proxy. Its gray connection therefore stops at the proxy. The proxy-to-pod segment is chosen per request. A dot shows each active request's progress; a ring marks the pod where a request completed in the current step.
Model boundaries
Requests have seeded, bounded durations. Pods are homogeneous, with no retries, failures, or session affinity. “Outstanding” means dispatched but not yet completed.
